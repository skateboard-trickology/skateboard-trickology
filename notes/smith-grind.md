# Title (current name)
Smith grind

## Description
grind on coping, ledge or rail on your back truck while your front truck dips downward below the obstacle

## History
Frontside version invented by Mike Smith in the eighties. Monty Nolder was the first to do a backside smith grind, named Monty grind.
